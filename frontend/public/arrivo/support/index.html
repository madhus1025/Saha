Arrivo Support

Contact madhu@wesaha.com for help. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and what you expected to happen. Please do not send passwords, payment information, precise home addresses, or your full alarm database.

What does Enable mean?

Enable turns on a one-time arrival alert. It does not ring immediately. Disable turns that alarm off. You can save and enable up to ten alarms independently.

Why does it say leave and return?

You were already inside the arrival area when the alarm started. Arrivo waits until iPhone detects that you have left, then entered again, to avoid surprising you with an alert when enabling it.

Why did I not hear an alert?

Arrivo uses standard local notifications, not a continuously ringing alarm. Check Settings > Notifications > Arrivo for alerts and sounds. Also check Silent mode, Focus, volume, Lock Screen permissions, and your audio output. A visible banner is not proof that a sound was audible. Detection or delivery may be delayed or missed.

What permissions are needed?

Enable Always and Precise Location for background arrival detection, and allow notifications. Foreground distance viewing can request location access separately. If permission is restricted by a parent, organization, or device policy, that restriction must be resolved outside the app.

Monitoring needs attention

Check location and notification permissions, Location Services, Background App Refresh, and Low Power Mode. Return to the app and use Retry on the affected alarm. Reopen Arrivo after force-quitting or restarting your iPhone. A saved alarm alone is not proof that iOS is monitoring it.

How does combined arrival work?

Arrival reports within five seconds can share a notification with their destination names. Stop acknowledges the included arrivals without disabling unrelated alarms. Each completed alarm stays saved but off until you enable it again. iOS controls delivery timing.

Why is distance unavailable?

Distance requires a fresh, sufficiently accurate foreground location. Try outdoors, enable Precise Location, and select Retry location. The value is approximate straight-line distance to the destination center, not driving distance or an exact boundary measurement.

Editing, deleting, and privacy

Editing an enabled alarm keeps it enabled with a fresh starting-position check. Delete removes only the chosen alarm. Arrivo does not keep a movement trail or automatically upload your saved places. See the privacy policy for Apple services, notification previews, backups, and voluntary support email.

Arrivo is still being prepared for public release. Do not use a development or beta build for safety-critical reminders.
