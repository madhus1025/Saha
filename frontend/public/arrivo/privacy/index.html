Privacy policy

Arrivo is operated by Madhu Avinash. Contact: madhu@wesaha.com.

Updated 2026-11-10

Information on your iPhone

Arrivo stores your saved destination names, coordinates, arrival radii, and alarm lifecycle information in its on-device app storage. It does not require an account or operate an app backend. We do not receive your saved alarms or location history.

Location and arrival alerts

With your permission, iOS monitors the arrival areas you enable. Always and Precise Location are needed for background arrival alerts. The alarm list and details can use foreground location to request approximate driving distances for saved destinations, including disabled alarms. Use my location requests this access in context. Distance-only updates stop when these screens are hidden, covered by setup or help, or the app leaves the foreground. Current fixes and route estimates are transient; Arrivo does not save a movement trail or add precise-location logging.

Apple services

Apple Maps provides map tiles, destination search and driving directions. Routing sends the current origin and selected destination coordinates to Apple to calculate the requested estimate. Search, map and route requests use Apple's services and network connections, governed by Apple's privacy terms. Choosing coordinates avoids a place-search request, but displaying a driving estimate still requires a route request. iOS supplies location and notification services. These platform interactions are separate from Arrivo collecting data on its own servers.

Notification previews

Arrival notifications can include destination names, including combined arrivals. Names may be visible on your Lock Screen or connected devices according to your notification settings. Sound-advisory notifications are generic and do not include destination names. You can change preview, sound and notification permissions in iPhone Settings. All notifications follow Silent mode, Focus, ringer and alert volume, and system delivery conditions.

Sound advisories

While active, Arrivo can read media/output volume, supported app-audio mute settings and notification sound permissions. Media/output volume is not notification or ringer volume, and an app-audio mute reading does not identify the Silent switch. Arrivo cannot reliably inspect Silent mode, Focus or ringer volume, or continuously observe changes while suspended. Readings are checked again when you return. Current advisory and notification-episode state stays in memory; no sound-setting history is saved or sent to us. Arrivo does not play audio, change your volume or keep background audio running to probe these settings.

No advertising or analytics

Arrivo contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, third-party tracking code, or in-app purchases. We do not sell personal data, build advertising profiles, or request permission to track you across apps and websites.

Your controls and deletion

Saved alarms remain until you delete them. Disabling an alarm stops its monitoring; deleting it removes its saved configuration and current alarm work. A notification already presented by iOS may need to be dismissed separately, and sound already played cannot be undone. Removing the app deletes its local app container. Device backups and restoration are controlled by iOS and your Apple Account settings, so copies may remain in backups according to those settings. Manage backup deletion through Apple.

Support and this website

If you email madhu@wesaha.com, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send through the email providers involved. No location, alarm database, or diagnostic log is attached automatically. Please avoid sending precise addresses or other sensitive information unless necessary. Support messages are used to answer your request and retained only as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. Our static website adds no analytics, advertising, cookies, or contact forms; the hosting provider may process standard network request information needed to serve and protect the site.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you voluntarily send us, contact Madhu Avinash at madhu@wesaha.com. We cannot retrieve or delete alarms held only on your iPhone; use the in-app Delete action. This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change, with the updated date shown here.
