A little less watching.
A little more arriving.

Choose a place, set an arrival area, and let your iPhone remind you when it detects entry. Keep your destinations organized without another account.

Public release in preparation. Free, with no ads or in-app purchases. An App Store download is not available yet.

Your places, together

Save up to ten alarms. Enable, edit, search, and delete them independently in a clear, scrolling list.

Know your distance

Open a place for its map, arrival area, and approximate straight-line distance. Distance updates pause when you leave the details.

Keep it personal

Saved alarms live on your iPhone. No sign-in, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or movement history.

One-time reminders, with clear limits

Each enabled alert waits for entry into its arrival area. If you are already inside, leave and return before it triggers. Arrivals reported within five seconds can share one notification showing destination names.

Important: detection can be delayed or missed. Sound follows Silent mode, Focus, notification settings, and volume; Arrivo does not ring continuously. Always and Precise Location are needed for background arrival alerts. Do not force-quit while monitoring, and reopen after restarting your iPhone. Do not use Arrivo for safety-critical reminders.

Requires iOS 18 or later. Map search and tiles may need an internet connection. Read the support guide or our privacy policy.
